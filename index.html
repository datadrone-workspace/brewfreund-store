Roasted in the Black Forest, sourced everywhere else.

Brewfreund started in a garage roaster in Freiburg with one goal: bring the kind of coffee we drank on origin trips back home, without the markup of a big-name café chain. Ten years on we still roast everything ourselves, in batches small enough that a roaster can taste each one before it ships.
